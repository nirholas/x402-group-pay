x402-group-pay — split the bill

The 6-person dinner problem: create a pool, share the link, each person pays their USDC share through the x402 payment modal, everyone gets a signed receipt, and the pool emits a signed funded proof.

Pay in USDC on Base or Solana — your wallet picks the rail. The modal reads both entries from the 402 challenge and offers EVM wallets and Phantom. Contributors to one pool can mix rails; the pool records which rail funded each share. Base Sepolia + Solana mainnet by default.
